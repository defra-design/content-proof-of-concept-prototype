AI assessment and reasoning

High overlap but distinct

The pages share substantial information about export health certificates, checking consignments, preparing documentation and meeting EU border requirements. Several passages use identical or closely related wording.

However, the pages have different apparent purposes. “Exporting fish to the EU” provides broader guidance for someone planning an export. “Preparing fish exports for EU border checks” focuses on the checks and preparation needed for a consignment.

The overlap may indicate that some procedural information is repeated unnecessarily. It does not establish that the pages are interchangeable or that one should be removed.
