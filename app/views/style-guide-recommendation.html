Terminology for food businesses

Draft proposal

Proposed guidance

Use “food business” when referring to a business that handles, prepares or sells food.

Use more specific terms, such as “food business operator” or “food establishment”, where they are required by legislation or are more accurate for the context.

Rationale

Different terms appear across content that may address similar audiences. Clearer guidance could help writers choose terminology consistently while preserving important legal distinctions.
