Terminology finding

Variation in terminology for food businesses

Similar concepts are described using different terms across Defra content. This may make it harder for users to recognise that the guidance relates to the same type of business.

What the analysis found

The analysis identified several terms used to describe food businesses across different pages. The terms may refer to the same or overlapping audiences, but the distinction is not always clear from the wording alone.

Why this matters

  • Users may be unsure whether different terms refer to the same audience.
  • Content teams may make inconsistent terminology choices when writing new guidance.
  • Existing guidance may not explain clearly when a specific term is required.

Existing style guidance

Before introducing a new rule, the content team should check whether the existing GOV.UK and Defra style guides already address these terms.

  • GOV.UK A to Z style guide
  • Defra Style Guide

AI recommendation

Consider adding guidance that clarifies the preferred terminology for food businesses and explains when more specific terms, such as “food business operator” or “food establishment”, are required.

This is a suggested addition, not an approved style-guide rule. The content team should confirm the terminology and any legal distinctions before deciding whether to add it.
